A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m {{ profile.name.split()[0] }}.
I pay attention.

I’m a designer working across digital products, physical environments, and spatial technology.

I make interactions that help people understand where they are, what’s changing, and what to do next. I care about the in-between moments: the sign that makes a place feel legible, the tiny decision that makes a product feel considerate, the interface that meets you where you actually are.

My practice moves between product and place. I bring research, prototyping, visual systems, and wayfinding together to make complicated experiences feel calm, clear, and a little more human.
